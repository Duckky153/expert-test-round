      Assumptions

      • Pass means the expert accepted the answer as written. A warranty or safety question only passes when the agent hands it to a person.
      • The agent's answers are pre-written synthetic text. No model runs on this page.
      • The manufacturer, models, part numbers, prices, documents and expert notes are invented.
      • The 90% bar for each expert is an assumption, not a known standard.
      • Due dates are assumptions, counted in days after the test round.
      • Marking an item resolved counts its questions as passing until the next round reruns them. Marks are kept only in your own browser.
